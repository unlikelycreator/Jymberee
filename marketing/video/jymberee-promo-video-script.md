# Jymberee – Promotional Video Script (Vertical 9:16, Google Flow, 3 × ~10 s)

**Title:** "Any emergency. One tap."
**Length:** about 30 seconds (3 clips of about 10 seconds each)
**Format:** **vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920**, for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status and Facebook Stories
**Tone:** urgent to reassuring. Real people, real homes and streets, no cartoon or 3D look. Nothing graphic: no blood, no injuries shown, no violence.
**Story in one line:** Emergencies can strike anyone: a medical emergency, a fire, a road accident, a theft. In every case one tap on Jymberee alerts loved ones and people nearby, and help arrives in seconds.

**How the 3 clips connect:**
1. **Clip 1** shows four different emergencies.
2. **Clip 2** shows the same red SOS tap in each of those situations, then the alert spreading across the neighbourhood.
3. **Clip 3** shows help arriving in each situation and ends on one warm, calm moment.

Everything happens in the same city at the same time (evening, just after rain), with the same colour look. That makes the 30 seconds feel like one continuous story, even though there are four situations.

## 3. Vertical framing rules

- **Keep the action in the centre.** Faces, hands and the phone stay inside the middle 70% of the frame.
- **Safe zones.** Leave the top ~12% and bottom ~18% free of anything important, because Reels and Shorts put the caption, buttons and profile name there.
- **Shot sizes.** Use close-ups and medium shots more than wide shots.
- **Camera moves.** Use push-ins, vertical tilts, crane-ups and rack focus. Avoid fast side-to-side pans.
- **Logo space.** The last frame of clip 3 leaves the upper third empty for the logo.

## 4. Google Flow prompts (copy and paste)

> **Before you start**
> 1. Set the aspect ratio in Flow to **9:16 (portrait)** for every clip.
> 2. **Recommended:** in Flow, first create one still reference image for each main character (Mr. Rao, Ananya, Kabir, Arjun, Meera, Mr. Sharma) from the cast descriptions above. Add them as **Ingredients** when generating each clip, so the same faces appear in clip 1, 2 and 3.
> 3. Generate the clips in order and use a short 4–6 frame cross-dissolve or a straight cut between them in Scenebuilder or your editor.

## 5. Tips for a clean result

1. **Montage clips are harder for AI video.** If Flow mixes up the shots inside a clip (for example, people from one scene appear in another), generate each short shot as its own clip with the same prompt text for that shot. Then trim each one to 2–3 seconds in Scenebuilder or your editor. This takes more generations but gives the cleanest result.
2. **Use Ingredients.** Character reference images keep Mr. Rao, Ananya and the others looking the same in clips 1, 2 and 3. This matters most, because the same people must appear again in clip 3.
3. **Aspect ratio.** Check that Flow is set to 9:16 before each generation. If a clip comes out horizontal, regenerate it rather than cropping.
4. **Clip length.** Flow (Veo) clips are often about 8 seconds. If yours come out at 8 s, the voice-over lines still fit, because each is under 7 seconds when spoken slowly.
5. **Generate 2–4 versions of each clip** and pick the one with the cleanest hands, phone screens and voice.
6. **Voice consistency.** With a montage, the most reliable approach is to mute the AI voice and record or generate the three narrator lines in one session (one voice, one take), then lay them over the clips. Keep the generated music and sound effects, or use one music track across all 30 seconds.
7. **Phone screens.** If a screen glitches, replace it in the editor with a real screenshot of the Jymberee SOS screen, or cut to a clean full-screen vertical insert of the app for 1–2 seconds.
8. **Name pronunciation.** If "Jymberee" is mispronounced, put the name only in your own recorded voice-over, or show it as text on the end card.
9. **Text and captions.** Add the logo, the tagline, the store badges and the captions in the editor. Optional on-screen labels during clip 1 match the app's emergency categories: "Medical", "Fire", "Accident", "Theft". Keep them in the middle of the screen, above the bottom safe zone.
10. **Export.** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264, about 10–15 Mbps.
11. **Claims.** The video shows only features the app has today: the SOS button, a WhatsApp alert with live location to loved ones, and push alerts to nearby users. Avoid showing earbud or shake triggers until they're confirmed working on both Android and iOS.
