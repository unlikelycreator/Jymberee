# Jymberee – Promotional Video Script (Google Flow, 3 × ~10 s)

**Title:** "Help, in seconds"
**Length:** about 30 seconds (3 clips of about 10 seconds each)
**Format:** 16:9, 1080p, cinematic and realistic (for YouTube and Instagram feed). For Reels/Shorts, generate in 9:16 and keep the subject centred.
**Tone:** tense to hopeful. Real people, real street, no cartoon or 3D look.
**Story in one line:** A young woman walking home alone at night feels unsafe. She taps the Jymberee SOS button. Her family and people nearby are alerted at once, and help reaches her in seconds.

## 2. Continuity rules (keep these identical in all 3 prompts)

- **Character (Priya):** Indian woman, about 27, shoulder-length straight black hair, light-brown skin, small gold stud earrings. She wears a mustard-yellow kurta, dark-blue jeans and a grey cross-body bag, and carries a black smartphone in her right hand.
- **Location:** a narrow residential street in Mumbai at night, wet from rain. Warm orange sodium streetlights, a small lit corner grocery shop on the left, parked scooters, a few apartment windows lit.
- **Lighting:** night, warm orange streetlight on her face, cool blue shadows, light reflections on the wet road.
- **Camera look:** handheld cinematic, 35 mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural film grain, true-to-life colours.
- **Narrator voice:** the same warm, calm, clear female narrator in her early 30s, with a neutral Indian-English accent and a slow, confident pace. It's voice-over only; nobody on screen speaks.
- **Phone screen:** show only a simple large glowing red circle (the SOS button) on a dark screen. There is no readable text, no app UI details and no other icons.

## 3. Google Flow prompts (copy and paste)

> **How to keep the clips joined:** generate Clip 1 first. For Clip 2, use **Extend** on Clip 1 in Flow (or **Frames to Video** with Clip 1's last frame as the starting frame). Then do the same from Clip 2 to Clip 3. Paste each prompt exactly as written below. They repeat the same character, place and voice so the model keeps everything consistent.

### Prompt 1 – "The moment" (0–10 s)

```

SCENE: A narrow residential street in Mumbai at night, wet from recent rain. Warm orange sodium streetlights, a small lit corner grocery shop on the left, parked scooters along the side, a few apartment windows glowing. Light drizzle, reflections on the wet road.

CHARACTER: Priya, an Indian woman about 27 years old, shoulder-length straight black hair, light-brown skin, small gold stud earrings, wearing a mustard-yellow kurta, dark-blue jeans and a grey cross-body bag, holding a black smartphone in her right hand.

She hears his footsteps, glances back over her left shoulder, looks worried, tightens her grip on the phone and lifts it slightly toward her chest. She keeps walking, breathing faster.

CAMERA: Handheld cinematic tracking shot moving backward in front of her, 35 mm lens, shallow depth of field, slow push-in on her face for the last 3 seconds. Natural film grain, true-to-life colours, warm orange light on her face with cool blue shadows.

AUDIO: Light rain, distant city traffic, her soft footsteps and slightly heavier footsteps behind her, a low tense music pulse. A warm, calm, clear female narrator voice-over in her early 30s with a neutral Indian-English accent speaks slowly: "It can happen to anyone... anywhere... in a single moment." Voice-over only; no one on screen speaks.

QUALITY: Clean, clear, natural voice with no distortion, no echo, no robotic tone, no stutter, no cut-off words, perfectly in time. Realistic human movement and stable faces. Consistent character appearance and clothing throughout. Smooth camera motion.

AVOID: on-screen text, subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks, readable phone screen, extra people in the foreground, distorted hands or fingers, extra fingers, warped face, flickering, morphing objects, jump cuts, cartoon or 3D-render look, overexposed lighting, music that drowns out the voice.
```

## 4. Tips for a clean result

1. **Clip length.** Flow (Veo) clips are often about 8 seconds. If yours come out at 8 s, the voice-over lines above still fit, because each is under 7 seconds when spoken slowly.
2. **Generate 2–4 versions of each clip** and pick the one with the cleanest hands, phone screen and voice.
3. **Voice consistency.** The AI voice can sound slightly different from clip to clip. If it does, mute the generated voice in your editor and record or generate the three narrator lines in one session (one voice, one take) and lay them over the clips. Keep the generated background sound.
4. **Phone screen.** If the screen still glitches, replace it in the editor with a real screenshot of the Jymberee SOS screen (track it onto the phone, or cut to a clean full-screen insert for 1–2 seconds).
5. **Name pronunciation.** If "Jymberee" is mispronounced, put the name only in your own recorded voice-over, or show it as text on the end card.
6. **Text and logo.** Add all text, the logo and the store badges in the editor (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci). Don't ask the AI to write text, because it usually comes out garbled.
7. **Joining the clips.** Use a straight cut or a short 6–8 frame cross-dissolve between clips. Add one music track across all 30 seconds so the audio feels continuous.
8. **Claims.** The video shows only features the app has today: the SOS button, a WhatsApp alert with location to family, and push alerts to nearby users. Avoid showing earbud or shake triggers until they're confirmed working on both Android and iOS.
